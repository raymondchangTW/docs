This page covers how exchange traded funds (ETFs) and leveraged and inverse (L&I) products trade on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK). HKEX groups ETFs and L&I products together as exchange traded products (ETPs), and they trade on its securities market.

<Info>
**Status of this page.** Last checked on 15 November 2026. It draws on HKEX's ETP trading arrangement sheet, which carries no date, and on an HKEX market making factsheet dated April 2022. Confirm trading hours, spread tables and fees against the current HKEX pages before relying on them.
</Info>

**Out of scope:** SFC authorisation and fund documentation for ETFs, HKD-RMB dual counter arrangements, and ETF primary market dealing. Related pages: [ETF market maker rules](/equity-derivatives/hk-etf-market-maker-rules) and [ETF creation, redemption and arbitrage](/equity-derivatives/hk-etf-creation-redemption).

## Trading hours

| Session | Time (Hong Kong) |
|---------|------------------|
| Pre-opening session | 9:00 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. |
| Continuous trading session | 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. |
| Closing auction session | 4:00 p.m. to 4:10 p.m. |

HKEX runs shortened sessions on certain half-day trading dates, so check the HKEX calendar for the day in question.

## Trading currency

ETPs can be traded in HKD, RMB (CNH) or USD.

## Spread table

Most ETPs use a dedicated ETP spread table with smaller ticks than equities. The lower bands are below. The full table continues to a tick of 1.000 for prices from 2,000.00 to 9,999.00.

| Price band (trading currency) | Minimum tick |
|-------------------------------|--------------|
| 0.01 to 1.00 | 0.001 |
| Over 1.00 to 5.00 | 0.002 |
| Over 5.00 to 10.00 | 0.005 |
| Over 10.00 to 20.00 | 0.010 |

At the preference of the ETF manager, some fixed income and money market ETFs use a separate table with a uniform tick of 0.05 for prices from 0.50 to 9,999.95.

<Note>
HKEX's reduction of minimum spreads in 2025 applied to equities, REITs and equity warrants. It excluded ETPs, whose spread tables stayed unchanged.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Conflicting descriptions of the spread table.** The HKEX ETP trading arrangement sheet refers to the equity spread table (Part A) for most ETPs and a uniform 0.050 spread for fixed income and currency ETPs. The market making factsheet refers to a dedicated ETP table (Part D). HKEX lists the applicable table for each ETP on its market maker page, so use that list for any specific counter.
</Warning>

## Short selling

ETPs are eligible for short selling with a tick rule exemption. The exemption applies to short sales below the best current ask price. Market makers have separate exemptions, which are covered on the [ETF market maker rules](/equity-derivatives/hk-etf-market-maker-rules) page.

## Settlement

- ETPs settle on T+2 through CCASS on a continuous net settlement basis.
- On 16 October 2026 HKEX published consultation conclusions that target a move to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The exact transition date is still to be announced, subject to regulatory approval and market readiness.
- Respondents to the T+1 consultation flagged the ETP primary market, rather than secondary trading, as the main readiness risk. See [ETF creation, redemption and arbitrage](/equity-derivatives/hk-etf-creation-redemption).

## Transaction costs

| Charge | Rate | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| Stamp duty | Waived | HKEX states that stamp duty is waived for all ETPs |
| HKEX trading fee | 0.00565% per side | Since 1 January 2023, when the HK$0.50 trading tariff was removed |
| SFC transaction levy | 0.0027% per side | No levy on securities market maker transactions |
| AFRC transaction levy | 0.00015% per side | No levy on securities market maker transactions |
| CCASS settlement fee | 0.0042% | Rate quoted by brokers from 30 June 2025, with the minimum and maximum charges removed |

Brokerage is separate and set by each broker. Market makers pay reduced trading and settlement fees, which are set out on the [ETF market maker rules](/equity-derivatives/hk-etf-market-maker-rules) page.

## Points to verify before relying on this page

1. The date and current content of the HKEX ETP trading arrangement sheet.
2. Which spread table applies to each ETP, using the HKEX list.
3. Whether the AFRC transaction levy applies to ETP trades. HKEX's cost page describes it for securities generally.
4. The CCASS settlement fee on the current HKEX schedule, because the figure above comes from broker fee schedules.
5. The exact T+1 transition date and its effect on ETP settlement.
