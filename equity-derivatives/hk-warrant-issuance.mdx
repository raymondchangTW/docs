This page covers two separate gates for derivative warrants (DWs) on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK): becoming an approved structured products issuer, and launching each warrant issue. The same issuer framework covers CBBCs and inline warrants.

<Info>
**Status of this page.** Last checked on 15 November 2026. It reflects the amended Chapter 15A of the Main Board Listing Rules (key product requirements from 1 May 2026, most other amendments from 1 July 2026). The product review guide cited below is the July 2018 edition. HKEX lists a June 2026 version of that guide, which could not be read when this page was written, so confirm the process steps against it.
</Info>

Related pages: [liquidity provider rules](/equity-derivatives/hk-warrant-liquidity-provider-rules), [trading rules and costs](/equity-derivatives/hk-derivative-warrant-trading-rules) and [CBBC rules](/equity-derivatives/hk-cbbc-rules).

## Overview

| Gate | What it decides | Who decides | Frequency |
|------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|
| Issuer approval | Whether the entity may issue structured products at all | Listing Division, with a Listing Committee hearing | Once, then continuing eligibility |
| Product launch | Whether a specific warrant issue can be listed | Exchange approval of the term sheet | Each issue |

## Part 1: Becoming an eligible issuer

### Eligibility requirements

The requirements below apply to issuers of non-collateralised products. A guarantor that meets all three tests can satisfy them in place of the issuer.

| Test | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Net asset value | Total shareholders' equity of at least HK$5 billion (previously HK$2 billion), based on the latest audited and interim financial statements |
| Regulated status | Regulated by the SFC for dealing in securities in Hong Kong, by the HKMA, or by another authority HKEX accepts. The earlier exception for government and government-backed entities was removed. |
| Credit rating | Investment grade from every agency that has been asked to rate the issuer. A rating under review for possible downgrade does not qualify. A holding company's rating may be relied on, subject to additional disclosures. |

Eligibility is a continuing obligation. Issuers and guarantors must keep meeting the tests while any of their structured products are listed. HKEX guidance states that an issuer that does not satisfy the credit rating requirement must not launch new issues, although obligations on products already issued, including liquidity provision and settlement at expiry, continue.

### Transition

| Applicant | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|
| Existing issuer or guarantor with non-collateralised products listed, or a valid base listing document, on 30 June 2026 | Until 30 June 2027 to meet the new NAV, regulated status and credit rating tests and the related continuing obligations |
| New applicant applying on or after 1 July 2026 | No transition. Must comply from the outset. |

An existing issuer ends its transition when it publishes a revised listing document that complies with the new tests. HKEX expects issuers to do this as soon as practicable, by issuing a new base listing document or an addendum, or through the supplemental listing document for each new issue.

### Application journey

HKEX describes the route to becoming an eligible issuer in three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Issuer preparation">
    Appoint a liquidity provider and prepare the application documents. Optionally consult the Listing Division on a confidential basis before filing. Then submit the listing application to the Listing Division.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eligibility review">
    The Listing Division accepts and vets the application. The Listing Committee holds a hearing. On approval, the applicant becomes an eligible structured products issuer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issuance">
    Prepare the listing documents and list structured products on the Exchange, launching each issue as described in Part 2.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The HKEX journey page does not state timelines, fees or form numbers for the issuer application. HKEX publishes an Issuer Eligibility Checklist and guidance for structured products issuers on its website. Chapter 15A of the Main Board Listing Rules has the full requirements.

<Note>
Appointing a liquidity provider is part of preparation, so the liquidity provision model should be settled before the application is filed. See [liquidity provider rules](/equity-derivatives/hk-warrant-liquidity-provider-rules).
</Note>

## Part 2: Launching a warrant issue

### Routine launch

For a product type the Exchange already permits, the issuer launches by submitting a term sheet and then publishing launch documents.

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Launch Date | The date the issuer submits the term sheet to the Exchange for approval to launch |
| Submission | Electronic. HKEX introduced electronic submission to shorten the listing cycle, and its e-Submission System supports batch submission for structured products issuers. |
| Listing cycle | Listing on the third trading day after term sheet submission. HKEX shortened the cycle from five to three trading days in 2020. |
| Documents | A launch announcement and a supplemental listing document for the series, read together with the issuer's base listing document, which is published on HKEXnews |
| Product terms | For a Launch Date on or after 1 May 2026, the issue must follow the current Product Sheet for its product type |
| Deviations | Issuers must consult the Exchange on terms that deviate from the Product Sheet or add features it does not cover |

**Listing date postponement.** Issuers' launch announcements typically state that if a disruptive event affects the underlying between the Launch Date and the scheduled listing date, the listing date is postponed so that two business days free of that event fall between them.

**Underlying in trading halt.** Do not launch structured products linked to a stock that is under a trading halt. The issuer should not submit term sheets on such an underlying.

**Recently listed stocks.** For a stock listed for less than 60 business days, the Exchange may permit issuance subject to conditions, for example a restriction on put warrants.

### New products, underlyings and features

Where the product type, the underlying or a feature is new, the issuer follows the Exchange's product review and approval process before launching. Issuers should consult the Exchange as early as possible.

| Stage | What happens | Typical timing |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Application | Issuer emails the Exchange with the supporting information, including a draft term sheet annotated for Listing Rule compliance and, for new features, examples, pricing, risks and hedging proposals | Exchange acknowledges within three business days |
| Approval in principle | Exchange assesses business, market, operational and Listing Rule viability, and seeks SFC comments. Granted only after the SFC has no further comments. | Exchange generally comments within 10 business days of the first submission and five business days of each later one |
| Documentation approval | Issuer submits the final listing document and related items | When the Exchange has no further comments |
| Formal approval | Issuer resolves outstanding issues and confirms earlier submissions remain valid | Generally within two business days |
| Launch | Once HKEX and market participants are operationally ready | |

Complex or new product types take longer. A product that needs a Listing Rule change or waiver may need Listing Committee approval and possibly market consultation.

### Eligible underlying assets

- **Local stocks.** The product review process does not apply. Issuers use the eligible stock list that HKEX publishes quarterly.
- **General principle.** Underlying information must be transparent and available to Hong Kong investors, and the price must not be controlled in a way that harms the investing public.
- **Overseas stocks.** The 2018 guide expects average daily turnover of at least HK$500 million, clear suspension and disclosure rules, and information in English or Chinese.
- **Indices, commodities, currencies and futures.** Assessed on the publisher or venue, calculation method, publication frequency and availability of information. HK$/US$ is not an eligible currency.
- **Unlisted underlying with knock-out features.** Issuers need feasible arrangements to suspend the product promptly when a knock-out event occurs.

## DW product terms

The terms below come from the HKEX DW Product Sheet marked "Last updated: 18 June 2026".

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Product class | Leveraged structured product |
| Style | European. American-style or other deviations require prior consultation with the Exchange. |
| Type | Call or put |
| Collateral | Uncollateralised |
| Expiry, new issue | Normally at least 6 months and at most 5 years from listing |
| Expiry, emulation issue | Normally at least 3 months and at most 5 years |
| Minimum issue size | HK$6 million market capitalisation at issue (previously HK$10 million) |
| Minimum issue price | HK$0.15 (previously HK$0.25). Does not apply to further issues. |
| Board lot, listed securities | One DW board lot entitles the holder to a whole number of underlying board lots, or to one-tenth of an underlying board lot (cash settlement only) |
| Board lot, index, currency or commodity | 10,000 DWs |
| Entitlement ratio, listed securities | Set ratios from 1 to 1,000 DWs per share, then multiples of 500 above 1,000; or one DW per 1, 10 or 100 shares |
| Settlement | Cash settled, with automatic exercise at maturity |
| Currency | HKD, USD or CNY. Trading currency and settlement currency must match. |
| Futures-referenced DWs | Expiry should generally match the expiry of the reference futures contract |

<Note>
The 1 July 2026 amendments added entitlement ratios of 2, 8, 20, 80, 200, 800 and 1,000 DWs per share, on top of the earlier 1, 5, 10, 50, 100 and 500. The prescriptive product terms were also removed from the Listing Rules. Issuers now follow the permitted terms in the Product Sheet for each product type.
</Note>

### Further and emulation issues

- A further issue is allowed only when the issuer holds no more than 50% of the existing issue, and its terms must be identical to the existing issue.
- An emulation issue replicates an existing DW. Its terms must be identical except for issue price and issue size, subject to the tolerance in the Product Sheet.
- The minimum tenor and the minimum issue price for new issues do not apply to further issues.

## Listing fees

The Main Board Fees Rules set a one-off listing fee for each structured product, payable on the application to list. The text found sets a HK$60,000 basic fee for the first issue an issuer launches in a calendar year over a particular underlying, with a different scale for later issues over the same underlying. HKEX may also run discount or rebate schemes. Confirm the current amounts in the Fees Rules before budgeting.

1. The June 2026 edition of the Guide on Product Review and Approval Process.
