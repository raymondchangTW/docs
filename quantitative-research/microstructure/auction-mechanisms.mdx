## 1. Call auction price determination

A single-price auction collects orders and selects the **equilibrium price** (IEP) that maximises executable volume. Standard tie-breakers: minimise the order imbalance, then choose the price closest to a reference. Orders at better prices than the IEP execute in full; orders at the IEP execute pro rata or by time priority depending on the exchange.

Why auctions matter: concentrated liquidity, a single reference price used for index, ETF NAV, and derivative settlement, and information aggregation at the open and close.

## 2. HKEX Closing Auction Session (CAS)

HKEX describes the CAS as applying to selected securities and consisting of four periods. For a full trading day:

| Period | Time | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference price fixing | 16:00-16:01 | Reference price (the continuous-session close) is set; no order input, amendment, or cancellation |
| Order input | 16:01-16:06 | At-auction and at-auction limit orders within +/-5% of the reference price; amendment and cancellation allowed |
| No cancellation | 16:06-16:08 | Input allowed; amendment and cancellation not allowed; price limit narrows to between the lowest ask and highest bid at the end of the order input period |
| Random closing | 16:08-16:10 | Same order rules as the no-cancellation period; the session closes at a random time within the two minutes |

On half days the same sequence starts at 12:00. After the random close, orders for CAS securities are matched at the final IEP. If no final IEP can be established, the reference price becomes the matching price and the closing price. Outstanding continuous-session orders within the price limit are carried into the CAS.

## 3. Implications for trading

- **Information:** IEP, indicative volume, and imbalance are disseminated during the CAS, so imbalance-driven strategies can react until the no-cancellation period.
- **Random close** limits last-second gaming and makes the exact close time unpredictable.
- **Benchmark flows** (index funds, ETF creation/redemption, futures settlement) concentrate in the auction. Basis and ETF trades that need the official close should plan CAS participation explicitly ([index and basis arbitrage](/quantitative-research/strategies/index-basis-arbitrage), [ETF arbitrage](/quantitative-research/strategies/etf-arbitrage-premium-discount)).
- **Cost modelling:** auction impact is not the same as continuous-session impact ([market impact and TCA](/quantitative-research/microstructure/market-impact-tca)).
