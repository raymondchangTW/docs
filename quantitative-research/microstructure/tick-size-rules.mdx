## 1. Why tick size matters

- The **tick-to-price ratio** (minimum spread / price, in bps) sets how binding the tick is. A large ratio means the quoted spread is pinned at one tick, queues are long, and priority is valuable ([queue position and fills](/quantitative-research/microstructure/queue-position-fill)).
- A smaller tick lowers the minimum cost of crossing, allows price competition, and typically reduces displayed depth at each level.
- Spread, depth, and the value of the queue all shift when the tick table changes; models calibrated before a change need re-estimation ([order book and spread](/quantitative-research/microstructure/order-book-spread)).

## 2. HKEX minimum spread reduction

Scope per the consultation conclusions: equities, REITs, equity warrants and other applicable securities; ETPs, debt securities, exchange-traded options, and structured products are excluded and keep their spreads. Prices below HKD 0.50 and above HKD 50 were out of scope.

**Phase 1** (launched 4 August 2025):

| Price band (HKD) | Old tick | New tick | Tick-to-price (bps), old to new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10.00 to 20.00 | 0.020 | 0.010 | 10-20 to 5-10 |
| 20.00 to 50.00 | 0.050 | 0.020 | 10-25 to 4-10 |

**Phase 2** (planned 2026): the 0.50 to 10.00 band moves from 0.010 to 0.005 (a 50% cut). HKEX stated Phase 2 would proceed if the Phase 1 review showed no undesirable impact; a January 2026 circular said the interim review found none. The HKEX page lists a Phase 2 launch date of 3 August 2026, and a later circular describes a 2-step rollout with confirmed dates to follow.

Spot check: HKD 15 at 0.02 is 13.3 bps; at 0.01 it is 6.7 bps. HKD 5 at 0.01 is 20 bps; at 0.005 it is 10 bps.

The conclusions also set the order price limit for non-ETP securities at 24 spreads or 5% from the reference price, whichever is wider.

## 3. Research designs around a tick change

1. **Event study:** compare quoted and effective spread, depth, volume, and queue length before and after, for stocks in the changed band versus a control band (below 10 for Phase 1).
2. **Difference-in-differences** with stock fixed effects; watch for concurrent events (the stock settlement fee restructuring was under consideration).
3. **Market-maker impact:** quoting obligations, inventory turnover, and realised spread.
4. **Strategy impact:** changes in fill probability and adverse selection for passive strategies.
