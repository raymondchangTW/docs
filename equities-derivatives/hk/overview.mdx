</Info>

## Venue and regulators

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Exchange | The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), operated by HKEX |
| Securities regulator | The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) |
| Clearing and settlement | CCASS, on a continuous net settlement basis |
| Other bodies named on the linked pages | The HKMA, for regulated status of structured products issuers, and the AFRC, which charges a transaction levy |

## Settlement

SEHK trades settle on T+2. HKEX published consultation conclusions on 16 October 2026 that target T+1 in the second half of 2028. The exact date is still to be announced.
