This page covers Northbound trading, which HKEX calls the China Connect Service. Hong Kong and overseas investors use it to trade eligible securities listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE) and the Shenzhen Stock Exchange (SZSE) through SEHK. Southbound trading, where Mainland investors buy Hong Kong-listed securities, is not covered here.

## Overview

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Programmes | Shanghai Connect and Shenzhen Connect |
| Eligible product types | A shares and ETFs. B shares, bonds and other securities are not included |
| Where trades execute | On the SSE or SZSE trading system. Northbound trading follows SSE and SZSE market practices and trading hours |
| Order routing | Orders go through the China Stock Connect System to the Mainland exchange |
| Trading and settlement currency | RMB |
| Clearing | HKSCC clears through CCASS and settles with ChinaClear |
| Trading days | A Hong Kong business day on which both the Hong Kong and Mainland markets are open |

The Mainland regulator, CSRC, supervises business under the Stock Connect rules, so Mainland rules apply alongside the SEHK rules.

## Eligible securities

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Shanghai Connect A shares | Constituents of the SSE 180 and SSE 380 indices, and SSE-listed A shares that have corresponding H shares listed on SEHK, subject to the criteria on the HKEX website |
| Shenzhen Connect A shares | Selected constituents of SZSE indices and SZSE-listed A shares that have corresponding H shares on SEHK, subject to the criteria on the HKEX website |
| STAR and ChiNext stocks | Open only to institutional professional investors |
| Other A shares | Open to Hong Kong and overseas investors, including individuals |
| ETFs | Eligible SSE and SZSE ETFs have been included since 4 July 2022, and are open to all Hong Kong and overseas investors |
| Excluded | Securities not traded in RMB, and securities under risk alert, are excluded from the A share indices-based eligibility |

HKEX publishes the full list of eligible securities, which changes with reviews. By the end of June 2026 there were 3,503 eligible Northbound A shares and 365 eligible Northbound ETFs. On 20 July 2026, 9 SZSE A shares and 46 SZSE ETFs were added as eligible for buy and sell.

A security that is recalled from the eligible list can only be sold and not bought.

### ETF eligibility and what it means for arbitrage

- ETFs are reviewed every six months for inclusion and exclusion.
- An ETF becomes sell-only if, for example, its average daily AUM over the last six months falls below RMB 400 million, or the weighting of eligible Northbound constituents in its benchmark index falls below a stated threshold. The threshold was 70% and was lowered to 55% in the April 2024 expansion.
- **ETF trading under Stock Connect takes place only in the secondary market. Subscriptions and redemptions are not allowed.** A creation and redemption arbitrage on a Mainland ETF therefore cannot be run through Stock Connect.

## Daily quota

- Northbound trading is subject to a daily quota of RMB 52 billion for each of Shanghai Connect and Shenzhen Connect, per the HKEX investor booklet of February 2024.
- The quota applies on a net buy basis. Investors can always sell their eligible securities regardless of the quota balance.
- Unused daily quota is not carried over to the next day.
- Once the balance reaches zero during the continuous auction, no further buy orders are accepted for the rest of the day. During the opening call auction, new buy orders are rejected once the balance is used up.
- The quota is shared between A shares and ETFs.

## Trading rules

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Hours | Follow SSE and SZSE hours. Opening call auction runs 9:15 a.m. to 9:25 a.m. China Connect Exchange Participants can input orders from 9:10 a.m. |
| Order types | Limit orders only, with a specified price. Orders cannot be amended. They can only be cancelled |
| Board lot | 100 shares for SSE main board stocks. Odd lots can be sold only |
| Tick size | RMB 0.01 |
| Maximum order size | 1,000,000 shares per order |
| Price limit | A general limit of 10% above or below the previous close. ETFs specified by SSE have a 20% limit |
| Risk alert stocks | The limit was 5%. According to a Hang Seng Bank notice, from 6 July 2026 the limit for SSE and SZSE main board risk alert stocks increases from 5% to 10% |
| Pre-trade price check by SEHK | A buy order may not be priced above 102% of the benchmark price, or 10 spreads above it if higher. A sell order may not be priced below 98% of the benchmark price, or 10 spreads below it if lower |
| Closing call auction | The same notice says SSE-listed funds use a closing call auction from 6 July 2026 |
| Day trading | Not allowed. Shares bought cannot be sold before settlement, and CCEPs may not conduct same-day turnaround trading |
| Off-exchange trades and transfers | Not allowed |
| Quota hogging | Artificial or bulk buy order input is prohibited |

## Short selling and securities lending

- Naked short selling is prohibited. Covered short selling is allowed only for eligible short selling securities, which are those eligible for both buy and sell.
- Stocks borrowed for short selling must be held in the selling participant's CCASS account before the start of trading on the day.
- Short selling orders may be input only during the opening call auction and continuous auction sessions, and must be flagged as short selling.
- Pre-trade checking applies to short selling orders.
- SSE and SZSE restrict stock borrowing and lending agreements. Agreements for short selling cannot exceed one calendar month. Agreements used to satisfy pre-trade checking cannot exceed one day, with no rollover.
- HKEX publishes the maximum number of shares available for short selling each day and updates the remaining balance every 15 minutes.
- Mandatory reporting applies to stock borrowing and lending and to short selling of A shares through Northbound trading.

## Settlement

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Securities settlement | Trade day (T). HKSCC runs two batch settlement runs at about 5:30 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. on T for each Connect market |
| Money settlement | T+1, around noon on T+1 on a net basis |
| Pre-trade checking | For sell orders, the shares must be in the investor's account with the broker or custodian before the order. Otherwise the order is rejected. Investors typically use a Special Segregated Account (SPSA) so the shares can be delivered to the selling broker |
| Market structure | HKEX describes Stock Connect as a T+0 market built into a settlement flow designed for T+2 |

## Foreign shareholding limits

Mainland rules cap holdings by foreign investors in a listed company. If the aggregate holding reaches the 30% threshold because of Stock Connect trading, HKEX identifies the relevant participant and requires it to sell down under the force-sell rules. A broker summary describes a sell-down to 26%.

## Fees and taxes

| Item | Rate | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handling fee (SSE or SZSE) | 0.00341% of consideration | Reduced from 0.00487% on 28 August 2023 |
| Stamp duty | 0.05% of consideration, on the seller | Reduced from 0.10% on 28 August 2023 |
| Securities management fee (CSRC) | 0.002% | Waived for ETFs, per broker schedules |
| Transfer fee (ChinaClear) | 0.001% | Waived for ETFs, per broker schedules |
| Transfer fee (HKSCC) | 0.002% of gross value of the trade | HKEX fee schedule |
| Stock and money settlement fees (HKSCC) | Nil for stock settlement. Money settlement fee waived until further notice | HKEX fee schedule |

The SFC investor compensation regime has covered Northbound trading since 1 January 2020. Capital gains tax treatment is determined by Mainland authorities and is not covered here.

## Investor identification and program trading reporting

- **Investor ID.** Under the Northbound Investor ID Model, participants must tag orders with a Broker-to-Client Assigned Number (BCAN) and maintain the BCAN-CID mapping file, including client type, with up-to-date information.
- **Program trading reporting.** The Mainland exchanges issued reporting requirements for Northbound investors engaged in program trading, effective from 12 January 2026. The guidelines require reporting before trading. Clients report through the participant that accepts their orders. Reporting is per BCAN, and account funding information can be reported centrally through a designated broker.
- **Proprietary trading.** On 17 July 2026 HKEX signed MOUs with SSE and SZSE for information exchange to support compliance for proprietary trading under Northbound Stock Connect.

## Points to verify before relying on this page

- **Daily quota.** The HKEX booklet of February 2024 states RMB 52 billion. An older broker page states RMB 13 billion. Check the current quota on the HKEX website.
- **Dynamic price check.** The HKEX quotation page for Shanghai Connect orders states a dynamic limit of 2% around the benchmark price in the continuous auction. Several brokers state 3%. Confirm the current figure on the HKEX page.
- **6 July 2026 changes.** The 10% limit for risk alert stocks and the closing call auction for SSE-listed funds come from a Hang Seng Bank notice that cites an HKEX circular. The circular was not read directly.
- **Trading hours.** Only the opening call auction and order input times were confirmed. The full session table, including the closing auction for SZSE and the change for SSE funds, should be taken from the HKEX trading hours page.
- **Fee schedule.** Broker schedules show a ChinaClear transfer fee of 0.001%. One broker shows 0.002%. The HKEX fee page text could not be read cleanly on which items are waived. Check the HKEX fee schedule.
- **Short selling reporting thresholds.** A weekly report applies when an open short position reaches RMB 25 million or a stated share of the issued shares. The figures come from a 2015 article and may have changed.
- **Foreign shareholding limits.** Only the 30% aggregate threshold and 26% sell-down level were found in the sources. The single-investor limit was not confirmed.
- **Block trading.** SFC and CSRC announced a plan for block trading under Stock Connect in 2023. Its implementation status was not confirmed.
- **ETF sell-only criteria.** The AUM threshold and weighting thresholds come from circulars of 2022 and 2024. Check the latest review circular.
- **Southbound trading** is not covered.
