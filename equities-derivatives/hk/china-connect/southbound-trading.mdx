Southbound trading lets qualified Mainland investors trade eligible securities listed on SEHK through Shanghai Connect and Shenzhen Connect. Orders from Mainland investors go to Mainland securities companies, which submit them to the SSE or SZSE subsidiary that routes them to SEHK.

## Overview

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Programmes | Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect and Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect |
| Eligible product types | Stocks and ETFs listed on SEHK |
| Trading currency | HKD. Only HKD counters of eligible securities are included. RMB counters were not included in the sources found |
| Settlement currency | RMB. Mainland investors trade in HKD and settle in RMB, so they take the currency conversion risk |
| Custody | The Mainland clearing house (CSDC) holds Hong Kong securities in an omnibus account at HKSCC for its clearing participants |
| Scale | Southbound average daily turnover was HK$118.8 billion in the year to April 2026, per HKEX |

## Eligible securities

| Category | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Hong Kong-listed stocks | Companies primarily listed in Hong Kong that are constituents of the Hang Seng Composite LargeCap Index or MidCap Index, or constituents of the Hang Seng Composite SmallCap Index with a market capitalisation of at least HK$5 billion |
| A+H shares | SEHK-listed H shares of companies that also have A shares listed on SSE or SZSE |
| International companies | Companies primarily listed in Hong Kong that are not Mainland issuers were added to Southbound eligibility under the same index-based criteria |
| Weighted voting rights companies | Included in Southbound Stock Connect, per HKEX and SSE announcements |
| ETFs | Eligible SEHK-listed ETFs, covered below |

In September 2024 Mainland investors could trade over 550 Hong Kong-listed securities through the two links, according to HKEX. Eligibility follows the Hang Seng Composite Index reviews, which are held twice a year. A company can be a Hang Seng Composite constituent without entering Stock Connect, as occurred in early 2026 for some stocks.

## Investor eligibility

A broker summary states that individual investors need an aggregate balance of at least RMB 500,000 in their securities and cash accounts to trade SEHK securities through Southbound. Mainland mutual funds are also allowed to trade through Southbound Stock Connect, per HKEX.

## Daily quota

- The Southbound daily quota is RMB 42 billion for each of Shanghai Connect and Shenzhen Connect, set on 1 May 2018 (from RMB 10.5 billion before then).
- The quota applies on a net buy basis. Investors can always sell their cross-boundary securities regardless of the quota balance.
- The aggregate quota was abolished in August 2016.

## Trading arrangements

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Trading days | Days when both markets are open for trading |
| HK trading sessions | Pre-opening session 9:00 a.m. to 9:20 a.m. Continuous trading 9:30 a.m. to 12:00 noon and 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., per a broker summary of SEHK hours |
| Pre-opening order types | At-auction limit orders. During the pre-order matching period from 9:15 a.m. to 9:20 a.m., Hong Kong Connect investors cannot input orders |
| Continuous session order types | Enhanced limit orders |
| Order specification | Buy or sell, price in HKD and volume |

Details of the order types and trading arrangements are in the HKEX Southbound education materials and the SZSE trade rules page.

## ETF Connect (Southbound)

SEHK-listed ETFs have been eligible for Southbound trading since 2022, and the criteria were expanded in 2024.

- **Review cycle.** Eligibility is reviewed twice a year.
- **Product requirements.** The ETF must be primarily regulated by the SFC, listed on SEHK and traded in HKD. A law firm summary adds that it must not be a synthetic ETF, leveraged product or inverse product.
- **Index weighting.** The 2024 expansion lowered the required weighting of SEHK-listed stocks and Southbound-eligible constituents in the benchmark index. A summary of the expansion states a minimum of 60%. The HKEX regular review document shows a 90% minimum for SEHK-listed stocks and 70% or more for eligible constituents of the main Hang Seng indices.
- **Sell-only.** An ETF becomes a sell-only security if it breaches stated criteria, which include a turnover ranking in the top 80% of shares by average daily turnover over the past 12 months, and concentration limits on the top five constituents.
- **Recent changes.** ETFs that hold US stocks were first admitted in November 2025. A further batch took effect on 6 May 2026, including Hong Kong-US equity ETFs. The number of eligible Southbound ETFs rose from 23 at the end of 2025 to 31 after the May 2026 review.

## Points to verify before relying on this page

- **Daily quota.** RMB 42 billion dates from 2018 sources (SSE and LegCo). No newer HKEX statement was found.
- **Trading hours.** The HK session times come from a broker help page. Check the HKEX trading hours page for current times.
- **ETF index weighting.** The HKEX review document and the later expansion summaries give different percentages (90% and 70% versus 60%). The review document may predate the 2024 expansion.
- **Settlement timing.** Only the custody structure and currency arrangement were confirmed. Settlement timing for Southbound trades was not confirmed.
- **Short selling and day trading.** Rules for Southbound investors were not found.
- **Fees and taxes.** Southbound fees and taxes were not confirmed for Mainland investors.
- **Investor eligibility.** The RMB 500,000 threshold comes from a broker page.
- **International and WVR companies.** Inclusion dates and conditions were not confirmed from HKEX sources.
- **Investor identification.** Whether a Southbound investor ID regime applies was not checked.
