This page covers how derivative warrants (DWs) trade and settle on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), and what they cost to trade.

<Info>
  **Status of this page.** Last checked on 15 November 2026. Product terms follow the HKEX DW Product Sheet marked "Last updated: 18 June 2026". The Chapter 15A overhaul took effect in two stages: 1 May 2026 for key product requirements and 1 July 2026 for most other amendments. No later Product Sheet was found at the time of checking. Always confirm against the current HKEX Product Sheet and the listing document of the specific issue.
</Info>

Related pages: [warrant issuance](/equities-derivatives/hk/warrants/issuance), [liquidity provider rules](/equities-derivatives/hk/warrants/liquidity-provider-rules) and [CBBC rules](/equities-derivatives/hk/warrants/cbbc). Product terms, which determine what can trade, are on the issuance page.

**Out of scope:** callable bull/bear contracts (CBBCs), inline warrants, and equity (subscription) warrants issued by the underlying company under Chapter 15 of the Listing Rules.

## Trading mechanics

- Orders are matched automatically on price-time priority.
- DWs use a separate spread table (code 06), which HKEX kept unchanged in phase 2 of the equity minimum-spread reduction.
- A DW cannot trade below HK\$0.01 in its trading currency.
- Overseas-underlying DWs are not automatically suspended when the underlying market suspends, so prices can move sharply until the Exchange suspends the DW.
- Liquidity comes from the issuer's liquidity provider. Quote obligations are on the [liquidity provider rules](/equities-derivatives/hk/warrants/liquidity-provider-rules) page.

### Minimum spreads

| Price band (trading currency) | Minimum spread |
| --- | --- |
| 0.01 to 0.25 | 0.001 |
| Above 0.25 to 0.50 | 0.005 |
| Above 0.50 to 10.00 | 0.010 |

This is the spread table that issuer sites publish for DWs. The sources checked did not show a price band above 10.00, so confirm the full table on HKEX.

### Short selling

DWs are not designated securities for short selling. Apart from the liquidity provider, it is not permitted to sell a DW you do not own and buy it back on the same day. The Rules of the Exchange provide specific exceptions for structured product liquidity providers and for structured product hedging participants.

### Settlement cycle

SEHK stock and warrant trades settle on T+2. On 16 October 2026 HKEX published consultation conclusions that target a move to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The exact transition date is still to be announced, subject to regulatory approval and market readiness.

## Expiry, exercise and settlement

- **Exercise.** In-the-money cash-settled DWs are exercised automatically at expiry. No notice is needed.
- **Cash settlement date.** Generally no later than the third settlement day after expiry.
- **Valuation, listed securities.** Average closing price of the underlying over the five business days before expiry.
- **Valuation, indices.** Settlement price of the related futures contract.
- **Last trading day.** The last trading day is earlier than the expiry day. Issuers must announce both dates at least seven business days before expiry.
- **Physical settlement.** Requires prior Exchange consideration and an exercise instruction from the holder.

## Transaction costs

| Charge | Rate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stamp duty | Not chargeable | Cash-settled DWs and warrants over non-stock assets are not subject to stamp duty. Stocks are charged at 0.1%. |
| HKEX trading fee | 0.00565% per side | Since 1 January 2023, when the HK\$0.50 trading tariff was removed |
| SFC transaction levy | 0.0027% per side | In force since 1 November 2014 |
| AFRC transaction levy | 0.00015% per side |  |
| CCASS settlement fee | 0.0042% | Rate quoted by brokers from 30 June 2025, with the minimum and maximum charges removed |

Brokerage is separate and set by each broker. The levies and trading fee are published by HKEX. Verify the CCASS settlement fee against the current HKEX schedule, because the figure above comes from broker fee schedules.

## Points to verify before relying on this page

1. The full DW spread table above HK\$10.00.
2. Trading session hours and the exact T+1 transition date.
3. The current CCASS settlement fee on the HKEX fee schedule.
