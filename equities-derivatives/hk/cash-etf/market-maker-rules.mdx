This page covers the market making regime for exchange traded products (ETPs) on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK). ETPs are ETFs and leveraged and inverse (L&I) products. The regime has two kinds of market maker: the Securities Market Maker (SMM) and the Designated Specialist (DS).

  **Status of this page.** Last checked on 15 November 2026. The eligibility, fee waiver and settlement fee sections follow the HKEX market maker page, which was updated on 9 November 2026. The quoting obligations follow HKEX circular ETP/003/24 (effective 1 November 2024). The monitoring and revocation rules follow an HKEX factsheet dated April 2022, which predates the 2024 change, so confirm them against the current HKEX arrangement.
</Info>

## Market maker types

| Item | Securities Market Maker (SMM) | Designated Specialist (DS) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | All ETP counters, covering ETFs and L&I products | Registered per ETP counter |
| Who | An SEHK Exchange Participant (EP) with an SMM permit | A corporate client of an EP, registered with the Exchange. A DS need not be an EP. |
| Entitlements and obligations | Set by the market making arrangement | The same as an SMM |
| Registration | An EP can register one or more DSs for a counter | A DS can be registered by more than one EP only for different counters. An EP may also make a market on the same counter as its registered DS. |
| Monitoring | By permit | By unique broker number |

### Eligibility and application

**SMM.** An SEHK EP must show it is suitably qualified under Explanatory Note EN10, covering financial standing, risk management, trading record, computer equipment and internal security. The application uses Form 10 with an internal report. Where market making positions sit in an affiliate's accounts, the affiliate must meet one of three tests:

1. It is a regulated entity.
2. It has a credit rating of A- (S&P) or A3 (Moody's) or higher.
3. It has paid-up capital of at least HK\$50 million and shareholders' funds of at least HK\$100 million at all times.

**DS.** The DS must be a client of an EP that is an SFC-licensed or registered Type 1 or Type 2 firm, or an overseas equivalent with an SFC memorandum of understanding. A licensed bank regulated by an acceptable authority also qualifies, as does an entity that meets the credit rating or capital tests above. The application uses Form 10A with an internal report under EN10A.

SMMs may apply for a Market Making Orion Central Gateway (MMOCG-C) session at a discounted rate, for themselves and for each DS.

## Quoting obligations

Market makers must enter two-sided quotes within the obligations for the ETP's group. HKEX publishes the group of each ETP in a downloadable list on its market maker page.

| Requirement | Group A | Group B | Group C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum spread, continuous trading session | 0.40% | 1.00% | 2.00% |
| Minimum quote size per side, HKD | 200,000 | 200,000 | 100,000 |
| Minimum quote size per side, RMB | 170,000 | 170,000 | 85,000 |
| Minimum quote size per side, USD | 25,000 | 25,000 | 12,500 |

| Parameter | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum quote period | 1 second after a market maker first enters an order |
| Minimum participation rate | 80% per trading day, across the pre-opening, continuous trading and closing auction sessions |
| Pre-opening counting window | 300 seconds, to the end of the random matching period |
| Closing auction counting window | 120 seconds, to the end of the random closing period |
| Applies to | Both SMMs and DSs |

Market maker participation in the pre-opening and closing auction sessions began on 1 November 2024. The Rules of the Exchange prescribe a range within which the maximum spread may be set, from 0.25% to 2.00%, with a 1-spread limit where the nominal price is below a level the Exchange specifies.

## Monitoring and consequences

The April 2022 HKEX factsheet describes the following regime. It was written before participation was extended to the auction sessions, so treat the details as indicative.

- A market maker breaches its obligations if it misses the minimum participation rate on five or more days in a calendar month.
- Each breach is reported to the SFC.
- A breach in two consecutive months may lead to revocation of the SMM permit for that ETP.

## Incentives

### Trading fee waivers

Waivers apply to SMMs and DSs on ETPs with a trailing 12-month average daily turnover of at least HK\$300 million. HKEX reviews the list quarterly.

| ETP type | Trading fee waiver |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic equity ETFs | 25% |
| Non-domestic equity ETFs and L&I products | 75% |
| Other ETPs | 100% |

The waiver tiers come from Exchange Circular ETP/001/21. The applicable rate for each ETP is in the HKEX obligations list.

### Transaction levies and settlement fees

| Item | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| SFC and AFRC transaction levies | None on securities market maker transactions |
| Settlement fee, market making exchange trade or manual trade | 0.0020% (0.20 bps), effective 30 June 2025 |
| Settlement fee, market making crossed trade | 0.0010% (0.10 bps). A crossed exchange trade is one where the buy and sell are handled by the same EP. |

### Stamp duty waiver

SMMs and DSs may apply to waive stamp duty on stock transfers made while creating or redeeming ETP units. Details are in EN10 and EN10A.

## Short selling and buy-in

- Market making trades are exempt from the short selling rules and the tick rule.
- SMMs and DSs can apply, through their Clearing Participants, for a one-day buy-in exemption on short positions arising from market making. The application deadline is 20:00 HKT on T+2.
- The Rules of the Exchange list securities market maker short selling among the exceptions to the short selling restrictions.

## Points to verify before relying on this page

1. The current HKEX market making arrangement document, including any changes to participation measurement and breach rules after November 2024.
2. The group assigned to each ETP, using the HKEX list.
3. Whether the 2021 fee waiver tiers have been revised. The HKEX page does not state a later revision.
4. Short selling and buy-in details, which the HKEX page does not cover beyond the buy-in exemption.
5. The effect of the future T+1 move on market maker inventory and the buy-in deadline.
