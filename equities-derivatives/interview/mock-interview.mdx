This is a mock interview for equities and derivatives trading desks (Delta-1, ETF market making and arbitrage, warrants and structured products). It is split into five topic pages. Each question has a main question, common follow-ups and answer points, and the answers are thinking frameworks rather than scripts. ## How a desk interview usually runs

Public guides describe a staged process: a short screen on motivation and basic knowledge, one or two technical rounds with traders, and often a final day with several senior interviewers. Options theory (Greeks, arbitrage, implied volatility), market questions, mental math and fit questions appear throughout. The mix depends on the desk, so a Delta-1 or ETF desk leans toward financing, basis and creation and redemption, while an options or warrant desk leans toward Greeks and volatility.
